The course has weekly exercises. There are _two types_ of exercises

- _weekly multiple choice quizzes_ that review the theory covered in the lectures and the course material; their deadline is on Sunday at 23:59:00
- the other part of the exercises deals with more technical topics of software engineering, such as version control, testing and software configuration; their deadline is _on Monday at 23:59_

How the exercises affect your course grade is explained in [Part 0](/en/osa0#grading-of-the-course).
