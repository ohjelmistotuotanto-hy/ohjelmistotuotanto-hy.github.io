### Separate exams

The separate exam can be taken in Examinarium from 2.1.2026 onwards. Register for the exam [here](https://examinarium.helsinki.fi/enrolments/109407?code=TKT20006_otm-e1ba1fdc-4cb9-436d-bb65-0ab50b0daf05). Before registering in Examinarium, you apparently need to register for the exam in [Sisu](https://studies.helsinki.fi/kurssit/toteutus/otm-e1ba1fdc-4cb9-436d-bb65-0ab50b0daf05/TKT20006)


**The same instructions apply to separate exams as to the course exam** (see below), except that no additional material is allowed. Since no material is available, the questions are somewhat different from the course exams of 2020 onwards, in which the questions have been more applied. 

If you complete the course with a separate exam, the mini project is not required.

### Course exam

The course exam is held on Tuesday 15.12. at 13–16 (exam time 2.5 hours) in halls A111 and B123. The exam is done the old-fashioned way with pen and paper. You may bring one A4-sized (both sides) handwritten "cheat sheet" to the exam.

The course has a total of 40 points, divided between the components as follows

- exercises 10 points
  - multiple choice quizzes on the lectures 2 points
  - weekly programming, version control and configuration exercises 8 points
- mini project 11 points
- exam 18 points
- attending guest lectures 1 point

Grade 1 requires 20 points, grade 5 requires 36 points.

In addition, passing the course requires an accepted mini project (or a credit transfer) and **at least half of the exam points**.

### Exam topics

The exam covers parts 1–5 of the course material and the exercises, except

- Git
- uv
- GitHub Actions
- unittest
- Robot Framework and the Browser library
- GitHub copilot

The guest lectures are not part of the exam.
