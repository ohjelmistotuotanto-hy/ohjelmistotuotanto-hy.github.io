### Course exam

The course exam is held on Tuesday 15.12. at 13–16 (exam time 2.5 hours) in halls A111 and B123. The exam is done the old-fashioned way with pen and paper.

The exam focuses on essay questions, i.e. you write your answers by hand in your own words. It is therefore more similar to the exams of 2019 and earlier than to the open book exams held in Moodle since 2020, where the material was available and the questions were more applied.

You may bring one A4-sized "cheat sheet" to the exam, with anything you like written on both sides. The sheet must be handwritten by yourself, so a printed or photocopied sheet is not allowed. Writing the sheet is also a good way to revise the course content.

The course has a total of 40 points, divided between the components as follows

- exercises 10 points
  - multiple choice quizzes on the lectures 2 points
  - weekly programming, version control and configuration exercises 8 points
- mini project 11 points
- exam 18 points
- attending guest lectures 1 point

Grade 1 requires 20 points, grade 5 requires 36 points.

In addition, passing the course requires an accepted mini project (or a credit transfer) and **at least half of the exam points**.

### Exam topics

The exam covers parts 1–5 of the course material, the material [AI in software development](/en/genai/) and the exercises, except

- Git
- uv
- GitHub Actions
- unittest
- Robot Framework and the Browser library
- GitHub Copilot and OpenCode
- the _Lisätietoa_ (more information) boxes in the material

The guest lectures are not part of the exam.

### Separate exams and retakes

The separate exam will possibly be available in Examinarium from 1.1.2027 onwards. More details and registration instructions will be added to this page later.

**The same instructions apply to the separate exam as to the course exam** (see above), except that no cheat sheet or other material is allowed.

If you complete the course with a separate exam, the mini project is not required.
