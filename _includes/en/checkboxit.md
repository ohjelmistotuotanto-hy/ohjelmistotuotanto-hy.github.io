### Checkboxes for tracking your progress in the exercises

The exercise descriptions of the course are sometimes long, and it can be hard to spot the steps that require you to do something. To make the exercises clearer and easier to follow, the steps that require action are marked with **checkboxes** instead of bullet points.

You can use the checkboxes to keep track of your progress. When you have done a step, tick its box. This way you will not lose track of where you are. The ticks are saved in your browser's local storage ([localStorage](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/localStorage)), so they are kept even if you reload the page. The ticks do not move to another computer or browser, and they are not sent anywhere.
