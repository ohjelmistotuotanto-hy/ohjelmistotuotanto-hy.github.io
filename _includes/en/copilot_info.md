Weeks 2–7 have [AI] exercises that you can do either with GitHub [Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) or with [OpenCode](/en/opencode/). Choosing the tool and the reasons for the choice are covered in [exercise 9](/en/tehtavat2/#9-first-contact-with-a-coding-agent-ai) of week 2.

You can do most of the exercises with the free Copilot Free version, but its monthly quota is limited. When done with Copilot, the week 5 exercises require Copilot Student, a version that is free for students and that you get by activating a [GitHub Education](https://github.com/education/students) membership. If you plan to use Copilot, **apply for the membership now**. According to the internet, it can take up to a week for the application to be approved.

OpenCode uses the language models of the university's CurreChat, so it does not require a GitHub Education membership.
